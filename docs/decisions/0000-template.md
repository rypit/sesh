# NNNN. Title in the imperative or as a noun phrase

- **Status**: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Supersedes / Amends**: (optional) earlier ADRs or constitution version

## Context

What forces the decision: the problem, the constraints, and which
principle, seam or threat-model assumption it touches.

## Decision

What we will do, stated so a reviewer can check the code against it.

## Alternatives

Each option considered, and why it was not chosen.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder, which invariants or documents change, and
any new risk accepted.
